A Top-Down Color-Magic Wave Shooter

📋 Overview

Role: Solo Developer (design, code, art & audio)

Play it: lostgaia.itch.io/chromatic-wizard

📸 About this game

  • Genre: Top-down 2D shooting
  • Setting: A wizard wielding color-based magical projectiles
  • Main Goal: Endure successive enemy waves for as long as you can
  • Core Loop: Fight enemies → Absorb their color → Grow your damage → Survive the next wave
  • Key Mechanic: RGB value is damage — absorb enemies' colors to increase your damage and reduce their resistance
  • Theme Tie-in: Built for the jam theme "Theft" — you literally steal the enemies' colors to power up

🎮 Core Gameplay (What the Player Does)

In Chromatic Wizard, you play a spellcaster whose magic is made of pure color. Every projectile carries an RGB value, and that value is its damage. Enemies arrive in relentless waves, each carrying colors of their own. Instead of simply blasting through them, you can steal their color — absorbing it to boost your own damage while weakening the resistance of foes that share that hue. The result is a fast, escalating loop where reading the color of the battlefield matters as much as your aim.

  • Color-as-Damage Combat: Your projectiles deal damage based on their RGB value, so the color you carry directly shapes how hard you hit
  • Color Absorption: Steal enemies' colors to increase your damage and reduce their resistance — the theme "Theft" in mechanic form
  • Wave Survival: Endure successive enemy waves that grow tougher over time
  • Strategic Targeting: Prioritise which colors to absorb to counter the enemies in front of you
